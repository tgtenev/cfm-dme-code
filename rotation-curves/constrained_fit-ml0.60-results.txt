THE CONSTRAINED FIT: THE BOOST LAW WITH a0 = c H_dS ln(1 + delta), kappa = 1, ON THE 153 SPARC ROTATION CURVES
c H_dS = 5.4185e-10 m/s^2 at H0 = 67.4 km/s/Mpc and 5.8687e-10 at 73 (Omega_Lambda = 0.6847); the scale relation gives a0 = 0.9426e-10 at delta = 0.19 (67.4) and 0.9214e-10 at 0.17 (73)
153 galaxies; 2000 bootstrap draws by galaxy, seed 20261114. Added Gamma tables (simple, standard): 0 points newly undefined; the model's Newtonian field agrees with that of aqual-refit-gamma-*.csv to 0.0e+00; the standard function's correction measured at a0 = 1.100e-10 m/s^2.
VARIANT: the mass-to-light priors are centred at 0.6,0.84 (disk, bulge) instead of 0.5,0.7, a test of the stellar normalization (Paper II, Sect. 6.1); rotation-curves/constrained_fit-optimum.csv and rotation-curves/constrained_fit-valley.csv are not written.
On the full field solution Gamma is read between the solver's nodes as in aqual-refit-fit.py: a quartic through the five free-volume
nodes (0.05, 0.2, 0.4, 0.7, 1.2), a Catmull-Rom cubic in log10 a0 on the 0.2-dex nodes at a0/s, and the correction for other
mass-to-light ratios linear in delta between its nodes 0.03 and 0.3; the constrained curve needs no interpolation of chi^2 between
scale nodes, since each of its nodes is fitted directly at its own a0. Part exact gives the error that the reading of Gamma adds at the optima.
