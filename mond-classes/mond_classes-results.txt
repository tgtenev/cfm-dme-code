A MOND-TYPE LAW AGAINST THE CLUSTER CORES, THE GROUPS AND THE WEB
==============================================================================================================
data folder: data
The law: MOND's simple interpolation function, nu(y) = 1/2 + sqrt(1/4 + 1/y) with y = g_N/a0, at
a0 = 1.2e-10 m/s^2 (mond_law.py). In the web, one prescription lets the field of structure act as an external field.

0. CHECKS OF THE LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  the inverse of the simple relation: largest relative error 4.4e-16
  the deep and Newtonian limits: g/sqrt(g_N a0) at g_N = 1e-16 m/s^2 is 1.00046; g/g_N at g_N = 1e-7 m/s^2 is 1.00120

3. THE LENSING OF THE WEB: THE BARYONS' FIELD OF STRUCTURE UNDER THE LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The lensing contrast is f_b nu_b(k, z) delta_m: the baryons, a share f_b = 0.0493/0.3152 of the matter, trace the
standard matter field delta_m of the Planck 2018 template, and nu_b is the law's boost of the baryons' own
Newtonian field, either per scale or with the rms field smoothed at 2 Mpc acting as an external field with the
QUMOND response. The distances and the lensing kernels use the template's flat background, Omega_m = 0.3152 (background.py).
  the per-scale boost of the baryons at k = 0.03 /Mpc: z = 0.5: 28.5, z = 2.0: 19.4
  the boost in the external field (independent of k): z = 0.0: 23.60, z = 0.5: 18.11, z = 2.0: 12.41
