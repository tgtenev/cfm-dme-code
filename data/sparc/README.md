# SPARC

The Spitzer Photometry and Accurate Rotation Curves database: F. Lelli, S. S. McGaugh and J. M. Schombert, "SPARC: Mass Models for 175 Disk Galaxies with Spitzer Photometry and Accurate Rotation Curves," AJ 152, 157 (2016), doi:10.3847/0004-6256/152/6/157.

The files were fetched on 2026-07-13 from the SPARC team's Zenodo record, doi:10.5281/zenodo.16284118, and are identical to the distribution at astroweb.cwru.edu/SPARC/.
They are redistributed here unchanged under their authors' Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.

- `SPARC_Lelli2016c.mrt`: Table 1, the 175 galaxies, with distance, inclination, luminosity at 3.6 μm, HI mass, flat velocity and quality flag.
- `MassModels_Lelli2016c.mrt`: Table 2, the mass models point by point: radius, observed velocity with its error, and the contributions of gas, disk and bulge, the last two at a mass-to-light ratio of 1 at 3.6 μm.
- `rotmod_ltg/`: the same mass models, one file per galaxy, with the distance assumed in each file's header.

`SHA256SUMS` gives the SHA-256 of every file.
