B. NGC 2419: THE NORMALIZATION OF ITS NEWTONIAN-SHAPED PROFILE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under ambient rescaling the whole cluster is rescaled by one factor P, one P per cluster, so its profile is
Newtonian-shaped with dynamical ratio Y_LC P. The shape cannot separate the two accounts and only the
normalization can. The Newtonian Jeans likelihood in M/L is computed from the multi-Gaussian
photometry of Ibata et al. 2011 Table 2, Osipkov-Merritt anisotropy from 2 to 500 pc and isotropy, the stability
priors, the 158-star window sample with a uniform contaminant up to 10 percent) and profiled over the anisotropy.
Photometric luminosity 4.6682e+05 Lsun. Window sample: 158 stars.
Newtonian Jeans fit: lnL -432.114 at M/L 2.150, r_a 18.9 pc;
68 percent interval of the dynamical ratio 1.83 to 2.60.

Globular clusters with well-measured masses show ratios of about 1.4 to 2.5 (Baumgardt, Sollima and Hilker 2020).
Those are Newtonian-dynamical ratios, so under rescaling they are not an independent stellar range. The needed
stellar ratio is therefore compared with the stellar value 1.8 and with the inner anchor of rescaling,
Y_LC = 3.15. At 49 pc the needed ratio is consistent with 1.8 within its 68 percent range. Inside the window,
58.254 to 200 pc, it lies below both 1.8 and the inner anchor.
Two conditions qualify this. Rescaling of the whole cluster by one factor is assumed. Were only a sphere about
the centre rescaled, the outskirts beyond 90 to 100 pc, about 8 percent of the light and the outer stars to 194 pc,
would lie outside the catchment and be unscreened. And the inner anchor of 3.15 is the
calibration of rescaling on the inner clusters, so it inherits the radial systematic of Part C.

C. THE CLUSTERS WITH RICH KINEMATICS: THE CORRELATION OF DYNAMICAL MASSES WITH P (CRITERION F-II.4)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sample: N_PM > 0 or N_RV >= 50 ('kinematically rich'), with a nonzero quoted error (luminosity-based masses
carry none). For these clusters the database's model reproduces the measured dispersions, so the catalog ratio
is a dynamical one. Each error is raised to the sampling floor sqrt(2/N), N = N_RV + 2 N_PM. Rescaling predicts
log Y = log Y_pop + 1.0 log P, Newton a slope of 0.
NGC 2419 enters with its Jeans ratio from B (2.15, 68 percent 1.83 to 2.60), since the database's model misses
its profile shape and its catalog ratio is 1.61. 'catalog NGC 2419' rows keep the catalog ratio,
and 'without NGC 2419' rows leave it out.
Velocities and proper motions per cluster in the sample: median 694; 18 clusters have fewer than 100.
