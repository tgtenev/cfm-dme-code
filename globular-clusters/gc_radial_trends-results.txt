THE RADIAL CONDITION OF THE GLOBULAR-CLUSTER PREFERENCE WITH A REALISTIC STELLAR M/L TREND
============================================================================================================
Delta lnL = max lnL(slope 0, Newton) - max lnL(slope 1, ambient rescaling); positive favors Newton.
Sample gc_dynamical-rich-sample.tsv; catchment lengths 58.254, 90, 100, 200 pc; single values at 58.254 pc.
Harris catalog mwgc.dat.txt, SHA-256 b457a690adc422fb2e8e05a23e1de50b62d1d5ac10dce99963c956c1b17ca658: [Fe/H] for 152 clusters (Part II), lg(tc) for 143 (Part III).

1. THE OBSERVABLE AND THE RADIUS OF THE CONDITION
   y = log10 of the catalog's GLOBAL dynamical M/L_V (Baumgardt N-body fit, segregation included), one value
   per cluster; x = log10 P(l_c); the radial trend is a covariate in log10 R_GC, the GALACTOCENTRIC radius,
   across clusters (0.7 to 96 kpc). It is not a radius inside a cluster.
   Pearson correlation of log P(58.254 pc) with log R_GC over the 133: 0.923.
